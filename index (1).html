We started with one small roaster and a lot of questions.

Ember Hollow began in a garage with a five-kilo roaster. We wanted coffee that tasted like the farm it came from, not like a dark roast covering every flaw.

Today we buy directly from growers, pay above fair-trade prices and roast in small batches every week. Every bag carries its roast date so you always know how fresh it is.

12 farmsBought directly, with prices agreed before harvest.
5 kg batchesRoasted Monday and Thursday, never stockpiled.
100% compostableBags, labels and shipping fill.
